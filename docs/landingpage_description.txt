Validation Landing Page & MVP Brief (V1)

Purpose

I would like to validate a new concept called Aging Parent Navigator before investing significant development time or resources.

The objective is to determine whether families are interested in:

- Assessing an aging parent's ability to live independently
- Understanding warning signs of declining independence
- Receiving guidance and recommendations
- Scheduling a consultation for personalized support

This should be approached as a validation-first project, not a full platform build.

---

Brand

Aging Parent Navigator

Working Tagline

Helping Families Navigate Aging with Confidence

---

Background

As a Registered Nurse with more than 30 years of experience, I have frequently observed families struggling to recognize when an aging parent is beginning to lose independence.

Many warning signs are subtle and often appear months before a major crisis.

Examples include:

- Unexplained weight loss
- Falls
- Missed medications
- Utility shutoffs
- Unopened mail
- Spoiled food
- Social withdrawal
- Memory concerns
- Bathroom accidents
- Declining hygiene
- Home safety concerns

The goal of Aging Parent Navigator is to help families identify these warning signs early and provide practical next steps before a crisis occurs.

---

Initial Validation Goals

Primary Goal:

Determine whether families will complete an independence assessment.

Secondary Goal:

Determine whether families are interested in a paid consultation.

Success Metrics:

- Assessment completions
- Consultation requests
- Email leads collected

---

Target Audience

Primary Audience:

Adult children (typically ages 45-65) concerned about an aging parent.

Examples:

- "Is Mom still safe living alone?"
- "Dad has changed recently and I'm not sure what to do."
- "I'm worried but don't know if I'm overreacting."
- "I need guidance on what steps to take next."

Development Philosophy

The objective is to keep this extremely lean.

I do not want:

- User accounts
- Memberships
- Complex dashboards
- Advanced platform functionality

For now, I simply want to validate demand and gather feedback from real users before deciding whether to expand further.
