> Rewritten 2026-10-28 around Sharon's decision to simplify: on-screen results only (no email, no
> saved assessments, no accounts, no database), Falls as the sole firm priority, "Not Sure" included

## 1. What this removes, and why the timeline changes a lot, not a little

Cutting email delivery, saved assessments, accounts, and the database wasn't a small trim, it removed
the single largest remaining block of engineering work. What's left is genuinely small: a disclaimer,
two short pages, and a final safety check. The honest result is a timeline measured in days, not weeks.

## 2. Scope for this launch

**In:**
- Falls & Mobility, live, pending Sharon's own testing and formal approval.
- Families complete the Concern Check and see their Family Guidance Summary on screen. Nothing is
  saved, emailed, or stored anywhere, exactly as requested.
- A privacy notice, terms of use, and a clear in-flow disclaimer.
- A final safety and functionality check before anyone is invited.

**Included only if ready in time, never a blocker:**
- "Not Sure" (`something-feels-different`) — if its content review finishes without slowing Falls
  down, it ships alongside it. If not, it ships later, on its own, with no impact on this date.

**Explicitly out, for now** (per Sharon's note that these return once the platform is established):
Caregiver Overwhelm and the remaining four pathways, email delivery, saved assessments, accounts,
analytics, scheduling/consultation booking, and anything else from the original Phase 2/3 roadmap.

## 3. Privacy and terms — simplest responsible approach

Removing the database changes this for the better: there's no stored data to explain, secure, or ever
delete, because none exists. That's a genuinely simple, strong privacy story, not a compromise.

**What I'd draft, at no cost, as part of finishing this phase:**
- A short privacy notice stating plainly that no answers or results are saved, transmitted, or shared;
  everything exists only in the visitor's own browser for that session and disappears when they leave
  the page. The only thing that touches a server is standard, non-identifying hosting activity, true of
  any website and not something particular to this one.
- Short terms of use: educational purpose only, not medical advice, no warranty, standard limitation of
  liability.
- The existing "educational, not medical advice, call 911 in an emergency" disclaimer, made explicit at
  the start of the Concern Check itself, not just in the footer.

**On legal review, as you asked me to flag before proceeding:** because nothing is stored, the legal
exposure here is meaningfully smaller than the earlier database-backed version. My honest
recommendation is still that a brief, light read from an attorney, even a short one, before real
families use something touching health topics, is good practice. But that's a cost and a decision that
belongs to you, not something I'll assume or initiate. I'll have the no-cost draft ready either way;
whether it also gets a paid legal pass before publishing is your call to make, and let me know what
you'd like to do once you see the draft.
